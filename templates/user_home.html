RECENT JOB SCAM WARNINGS
WARNING #1

The "Registration Fee" Trap

Real employers never demand payment for training kits, application forms, software installation, or security deposits.

Safety Tip: Never pay money to receive a job offer.
WARNING #2

Interviews on Telegram & WhatsApp

Be careful when recruiters refuse official video calls or corporate email threads and insist on chatting strictly via messaging apps.

Safety Tip: Always verify job listings on the company’s official careers page.
WARNING #3

Too-Good-To-Be-True Salaries

Promising ₹50,000/week or $500/day for simple typing or copy-paste tasks with no experience required is a classic sign of fraud.

Safety Tip: Check market rates for entry-level jobs before applying.

📋 Quick Safety Checklist for Candidates

Keep these four rules in mind whenever you receive an unsolicited job email:

✓
Check the Sender Email

Legitimate recruiters use official corporate emails, not free Gmail or Yahoo accounts.

✓
Never Pay Any Upfront Fee

Genuine companies pay you; they will never ask you to pay for job applications or laptops.

✓
Verify on Career Websites

Check the company's official careers portal to see if the job vacancy actually exists.

✓
Protect Personal Details

Never share bank account details, UPI passwords, or IDs before signing a verified contract.
